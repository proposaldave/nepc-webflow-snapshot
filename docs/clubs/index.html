The people behind NEPC

Come for the game.
Stay for the people.

Samara and Dave want NEPC to be a place you look forward to coming back to—for the pickleball and the people. They’re building that experience alongside the staff and members who make each club its own community.

Each club has six courts: named after the New England states in Rye, and the state capitals in Middleton.
